# NEXORA / TEKNOVA Electronics — Shopify OS 2.0 Theme

Ultra-premium, editorial hardware & electronics flagship Shopify theme built on Shopify Online Store 2.0 architecture.

## Overview
- **Design Archetype**: Apple / Nothing inspired high-end consumer hardware aesthetic.
- **Typography**: Manrope (Headlines & Display) and Hanken Grotesk (Body & Labels).
- **Color Palette**: Electric Violet (`#7c3aed`), Royal Blue (`#0051d5`), and Crisp White / Obsidian Dark contrast.
- **Icons**: Google Material Symbols Outlined.
- **Responsive**: Fully responsive across Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop layouts.

## Sections Included
1. **Announcement Bar** (`announcement-bar.liquid`): Multi-message cycling ticker with currency and utility links.
2. **Sticky Header** (`header.liquid`): Integrated search, navigation, wishlist, and dynamic cart drawer count.
3. **Hero Banner** (`hero-banner.liquid`): Editorial composition with floating spec badges and trust highlights.
4. **Shop by Categories** (`shop-by-categories.liquid`): 6 Engineered hardware category cards with hover zoom.
5. **Trending Products** (`trending-products.liquid`): 5-column product grid with interactive category filter tabs.
6. **Promotional Banner** (`promotional-banner.liquid`): High-contrast audio architecture banner with VIP perks.
7. **Best Sellers** (`best-sellers.liquid`): Customer favorites grid with urgency stock badges.
8. **New Arrivals** (`new-arrivals.liquid`): Hardware release reel with navigation controls.
9. **Flash Deal Drop** (`flash-deal.liquid`): 48-hour countdown timer with live inventory remaining meter.
10. **Why Shop With Us** (`why-shop-with-us.liquid`): 4 brand guarantee cards (Genuine, Secure, Insured, 7-Day Replacement).
11. **Brand Partners** (`brand-partners.liquid`): Authorized distribution ribbon (Apple, Samsung, Sony, etc.).
12. **Customer Reviews** (`customer-reviews.liquid`): 4.9-star rating summary and verified buyer cards.
13. **Newsletter VIP Drop** (`newsletter.liquid`): VIP drop email form with instant unlock coupon reward.
14. **FAQ Accordion** (`faq-accordion.liquid`): Smooth details/summary accordion for customer support.
15. **Social Gallery** (`social-gallery.liquid`): Instagram setup showcase with hover interaction.
16. **Mega Footer** (`footer.liquid`): 6-column footer with payment gateway badges and policy links.

## Shopify Online Store 2.0 Core Templates
- `templates/index.json`: Pre-configured homepage schema.
- `templates/product.json` & `sections/main-product.liquid`: Full product detail page with image gallery and variant options.
- `templates/collection.json` & `sections/main-collection.liquid`: Product listing grid with sorting and pagination.
- `templates/cart.json` & `sections/main-cart.liquid`: Shopping cart with live order summary.
- `templates/search.json` & `sections/main-search.liquid`: Live search results page.
- `templates/page.json` & `sections/main-page.liquid`: Standard rich-text pages.
- `templates/404.json` & `sections/main-404.liquid`: 404 signal lost page.

## Connecting to Shopify Store
1. Go to your Shopify Store Admin > **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Click **Add theme > Connect from GitHub**.
3. Select this repository (`baghelrahul/teknova-electronics-theme`) and choose the branch `main`.
4. Click **Connect theme**.
